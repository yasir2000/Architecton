# Architecton brand

## Name and tagline

- **Name:** Architecton (from the Greek _ἀρχιτέκτων_, the master builder). Write it with a capital A and no spaces. Use the Greek form only as a subtitle.
- **Tagline:** _From human intentions to verifiable executable systems._

## Logo

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [`assets/logo.svg`](../assets/logo.svg) | Primary logo. Use everywhere vector images work. |
| [`assets/logo.png`](../assets/logo.png) | 512×512 raster for social previews, avatars and slides. |

The mark is a stone arch with a gold keystone framing a green check mark: a structure whose correctness is verified. Keep clear space around it of at least one eighth of its width. Don't recolour, stretch or add effects to it.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary (background, headings) | Deep indigo | `#1E2A5A` |
| Accent (keystone, base) | Gold | `#D4A72C` |
| Verified / success | Green | `#3CC48F` |
| Light (stone, text on indigo) | Mist | `#E8ECF7` |

## Voice

Precise and honest about limits. State what is proved, what is tested and what is assumed. Never call model-level proofs "verified software".
